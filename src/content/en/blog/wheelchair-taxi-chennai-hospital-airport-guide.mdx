Chennai journeys can involve busy roads, long distances between neighbourhoods, and multiple entrances at hospitals, airports, and railway stations. If you are arranging a wheelchair-accessible taxi for yourself or a family member, a few details shared ahead of time can help the ride go more smoothly.

## Wheelchair taxi for Chennai hospital visits

When arranging a wheelchair cab for an appointment, provide the hospital or clinic name, department if known, appointment time, and the entrance or drop-off point you have been asked to use. You can enquire about trips to facilities such as Apollo Hospitals, MIOT International, Kauvery Hospital, MGM Healthcare, Fortis Malar Hospital, and other Chennai hospitals. These are examples of destinations only; the taxi service is not affiliated with these providers.

For dialysis, physiotherapy, follow-up appointments, or a planned discharge, say whether you need a one-way trip, a return pickup, waiting time, or recurring bookings. Confirm the fare and trip details before travel.

## Chennai airport and railway transfers

For a wheelchair taxi to Chennai International Airport, share your flight number, terminal, pickup time, luggage needs, and whether you are arriving or departing. For a station trip, specify Chennai Central or Egmore and the exact entrance or pickup point. Allow extra time for traffic, boarding, and finding the correct meeting location.

An airport transfer or railway cab may involve more luggage and companions than a short local trip. Tell the booking team how many people will travel and ask whether the requested wheelchair and baggage will fit the vehicle.

## What to tell the cab provider before you book

1. Pickup address and destination, including apartment or hospital entrance details.
2. Wheelchair type and dimensions, plus any assistance needed for boarding.
3. Number of accompanying family members or caregivers.
4. Appointment, flight, or train time and your preferred pickup time.
5. Whether a return ride, a waiting period, or a repeat booking is needed.

Accessible vehicles are not all identical. Ask whether the specific wheelchair taxi has the lift or ramp you need and whether the passenger can remain in their chair for the journey. Do not rely on a general label such as "accessible cab" without confirming the details.

## Chennai areas and local pickup planning

You can ask about wheelchair cab pickups in areas including Adyar, Anna Nagar, Guindy, Nungambakkam, OMR, Perambur, Porur, Tambaram, T. Nagar, and Velachery. Share the exact address and destination when requesting availability; distance, traffic, and vehicle scheduling affect each booking.

## A wheelchair taxi is not an ambulance

A wheelchair cab is intended for planned trips and does not provide medical monitoring or emergency care. If the passenger is unwell, needs oxygen or clinical assistance en route, or needs urgent medical attention, ask their healthcare provider about appropriate transport or contact emergency services.
